# Support H Model research

GitHub Sponsors is the selected channel for voluntary research support. The intended recipient is the project owner's verified GitHub account, [hutkobut-gif](https://github.com/hutkobut-gif). Sponsor enrollment, approval, payout readiness and a live sponsorship page have not been verified. This document is preparation, not an announcement that payments are available.

Planned support may help maintain simulations, improve reproducibility packages, review historical evidence and prepare careful documentation. Allocation and sponsorship tier prices must be confirmed by the owner before accepting support. Negative and inconclusive findings remain valid research outputs.

Sponsorship does not guarantee a breakthrough, physical hardware capability, commercial product, a delivery date or a successful experiment. It does not purchase intellectual-property rights, a software license or access to withheld research. Funding must not change scientific verdicts, stop rules or publication review.

Do not put payment, identity, tax or banking records in this repository. Sponsor acknowledgments should respect the sponsor's consent and platform privacy settings.

The repository funding configuration identifies the intended GitHub Sponsors recipient. It does not establish account eligibility or payment readiness. The repository remains private until the owner authorizes public release.
