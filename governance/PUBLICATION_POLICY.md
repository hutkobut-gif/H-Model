# Publication policy

Every source artifact receives exactly one classification.

- PUBLIC: reviewed material safe to stage now.
- REVIEW_BEFORE_PUBLIC: potentially releasable after scientific, IP and security review; excluded from this release.
- HOLD: unresolved validation, IP, novelty, safety, strategic or reconstruction exposure; excluded from this release.
- PRIVATE: internal material with no intended publication; excluded from this release.
- EXCLUDE: credentials, caches, temporary/generated infrastructure and irrelevant files; excluded from this release.

Uncertainty defaults to HOLD. A matching content term is a review signal, not proof of sensitivity or scientific success. Indirect dependencies capable of reconstructing withheld work receive the same protection. Original experiment folders remain unchanged; the internal inventory retains paths, reasons and fingerprints without duplicating raw data.

Some branches remain withheld pending validation and IP/scientific review. HOLD does not mean success; invalid studies must never be cited as validated evidence. Published simulation results are not physical proof.

Released artifacts carry source identifiers and hashes where available. Sanitization is recorded. Post-hoc analyses and diagnostics retain their labels. A private GitHub upload is a staging step; owner approval is required before public visibility. License choice remains an owner decision.
