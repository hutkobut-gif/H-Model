# Scientific methodology

Define the hypothesis, baseline, evaluation seeds, effect size, controls, validity checks and stop rule before evaluation. Freeze and hash the preregistration, source and configuration. Keep calibration distinct from evaluation. Record deviations with their timing; do not retrospectively call a post-hoc discovery preregistered evidence.

Separate operational completion from scientific validity. A completed simulation may have failed or invalid controls. Publish negative findings with their limits and preserve unrun stages. Treat positive exploratory observations as hypotheses for a new validation study.

Simulation performance does not establish physical feasibility, energy efficiency, safety or hardware advantage. These require separate evidence.
