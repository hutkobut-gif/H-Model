# H Model

## Research goal
H Model investigates whether local dynamics and state in physical-computing abstractions provide useful computational capabilities beyond strong conventional baselines.

## Scientific approach
The project uses falsification-first tests, preregistered gates, calibration/evaluation separation, negative controls, conventional comparators, deviation ledgers and stop rules. Hypotheses are separated from demonstrated capabilities.

## What has been tested
This release summarizes seven historical simulation tracks: v0, v1a, v2, a two-cell test, adaptive sensors, mechanical morphology and ionic/chemical separation. The summaries retain failed gates, stopped stages and non-counting diagnostics.

## Current evidence level
The evidence presented here is numerical simulation reported by the project. No independent rerun, peer review or physical hardware validation is claimed. The principal findings are negative or mixed. No claim of achieved AGI, new physics, commercial readiness, patentability or established novelty is made.

## Experiment structure
Each released track has a sanitized summary under `experiments/`. Fingerprints and scope are recorded in `results/PROVENANCE.json`. Source reports, raw results and detailed implementation packages remain outside this repository pending review.

## Reproducibility
This is a documentation release. It supports identification of source artifacts by SHA-256, but it does not contain the files needed to independently rerun the experiments. Publication of reviewed code, configurations, preregistrations, data and environment locks is a future requirement. See `docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`.

## Published research tracks
The seven folders under `experiments/` contain historical summaries, not runnable experiment packages. See `docs/EVIDENCE_SCOPE.md` for outcomes and limits.

## Withheld research branches
Some experimental side branches are temporarily withheld pending additional validation, IP review, or scientific review. HOLD does not mean success.

## Limitations
Conclusions apply to the tested simulations, baselines and budgets. Source hash consistency does not independently establish preregistration timing. Smoke runs are not full scientific completion. Later frontier work is not represented as validated evidence in this release.

## Safety / responsible research
Keep credentials, personal information and internal infrastructure out of publication. Audit evidence claims and compare against strong conventional baselines. Report invalid, failed and inconclusive studies accurately.

## Roadmap
Review dependencies and IP exposure; verify historical provenance; release complete reproducibility packages where authorized; improve controls before further capability claims; assess any hardware separately.
